# What are LAN, MAN, and WAN?

**LAN, MAN, and WAN** are different types of **computer networks**. They are classified based on the **area they cover**.

**Simple Definition:**
> **LAN, MAN, and WAN are types of computer networks used to connect computers and devices over different geographical areas.**

---

# 1. LAN (Local Area Network)

A **LAN (Local Area Network)** is a network that connects computers within a **small area**, such as a home, school, office, or laboratory.

**Simple Definition:**
> **LAN is a network that connects devices within a small area.**

### Examples
- Home Wi-Fi network
- School computer lab
- Office network
- Cyber café

### Features
- Covers a small area
- High data transfer speed
- Easy to install and maintain
- Low cost
- Privately owned

### Advantages
- Fast communication
- Easy file sharing
- Resource sharing (printers, scanners)
- Low maintenance cost
- Better security

### Disadvantages
- Limited coverage
- Requires network equipment
- Not suitable for long distances

---

# 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

A **MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)** connects multiple LANs within a **city or metropolitan area**.

**Simple Definition:**
> **MAN is a network that connects computers across a city or large campus.**

### Examples
- University campus network
- City government offices
- Cable TV network
- Bank branches within a city

### Features
- Covers a city or large campus
- Faster than WAN
- Connects multiple LANs
- Medium installation cost

### Advantages
- Covers a larger area than LAN
- High-speed communication
- Efficient data sharing across a city
- Connects many offices

### Disadvantages
- More expensive than LAN
- More complex to manage
- Requires specialized networking equipment

---

# 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)

A **WAN (Wide Area Network)** connects computers over **large geographical areas**, such as countries or continents.

**Simple Definition:**
> **WAN is a network that connects computers over long distances.**

### Examples
- The Internet
- Bank networks across India
- Airline reservation systems
- International company networks

### Features
- Covers countries or continents
- Connects multiple LANs and MANs
- Uses public and private communication lines
- Supports global communication

### Advantages
- Worldwide connectivity
- Easy communication across long distances
- Supports remote work
- Centralized data access

### Disadvantages
- Higher cost
- Slower than LAN
- More difficult to manage
- Greater security challenges

---

# Comparison of LAN, MAN, and WAN

| Feature | LAN | MAN | WAN |
|---------|-----|-----|-----|
| Full Form | Local Area Network | Metropolitan Area Network | Wide Area Network |
| Coverage Area | Small area | City or large campus | Country or worldwide |
| Speed | High | Medium to High | Medium |
| Cost | Low | Medium | High |
| Ownership | Private | Private or Public | Public or Private |
| Example | Home, School, Office | University Campus | Internet |
