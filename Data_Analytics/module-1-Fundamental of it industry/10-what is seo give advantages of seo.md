# What is SEO?

**SEO** stands for **Search Engine Optimization**.

SEO is the process of **improving a website so that it appears higher in search engine results** like Google, Bing, and Yahoo.

**Simple Definition:**
> **SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of improving a website to increase its visibility and ranking in search engine results.**

**Example:**
If someone searches for **"best mobile phones"** on Google, websites with good SEO are more likely to appear on the first page.

---

# Why is SEO Used?

SEO is used to:

- Increase website visitors
- Improve search engine ranking
- Attract the right audience
- Build trust and credibility
- Increase sales and leads

---

# How Does SEO Work?

1. Create useful and high-quality content.
2. Use relevant keywords.
3. Improve website speed.
4. Make the website mobile-friendly.
5. Build quality backlinks.
6. Search engines rank the website based on these factors.

---

# Types of SEO

## 1. On-Page SEO

On-Page SEO focuses on optimizing the content and pages of a website.

**Examples:**
- Using keywords
- Writing good titles
- Optimizing images
- Creating quality content
- Internal linking

---

## 2. Off-Page SEO

Off-Page SEO focuses on activities outside the website.

**Examples:**
- Backlinks
- Social media sharing
- Guest blogging
- Brand mentions

---

## 3. Technical SEO

Technical SEO improves the technical performance of a website.

**Examples:**
- Website speed
- Mobile-friendly design
- HTTPS security
- XML Sitemap
- Robots.txt

---

## 4. Local SEO

Local SEO helps businesses appear in local search results.

**Examples:**
- Google Business Profile
- Local keywords
- Customer reviews
- Maps listing
