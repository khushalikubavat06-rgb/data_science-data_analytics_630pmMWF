# What is Google Business Profile?

**Google Business Profile (formerly called Google My Business)** is a free tool provided by Google that helps businesses manage how they appear on **Google Search** and **Google Maps**.

**Simple Definition:**
> **Google Business Profile is a free Google service that allows businesses to create an online profile so customers can easily find their business, contact them, and view important information.**

**Example:**
If you search for **"coffee shop near me"** on Google, the business information (name, address, phone number, reviews, photos, and timings) shown on the right side or in Google Maps comes from its **Google Business Profile**.

---

# Why is Google Business Profile Used?

It helps businesses:

- Increase online visibility
- Appear on Google Search and Google Maps
- Share business information
- Receive customer reviews
- Attract more customers
- Build trust and credibility

# Steps to Create a Google Business Profile

1. Sign in with your Google Account.
2. Go to **Google Business Profile**.
3. Click **Manage Now** or **Add your business**.
4. Enter your business name.
5. Select the business category.
6. Add the business address.
7. Enter the phone number and website.
8. Verify your business.
9. Add business hours, photos, and services.
10. Publish your profile.
