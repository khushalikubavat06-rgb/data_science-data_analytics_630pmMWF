# What is WWW?

**WWW** stands for **World Wide Web**.

It is a collection of **websites and web pages** that can be accessed through the **Internet** using a **web browser**.

**Simple Definition:**
> **The World Wide Web (WWW) is a system of interconnected web pages and websites that people can access using a web browser over the Internet.**

**Example:**

- https://www.google.com
- https://www.wikipedia.org
- https://www.youtube.com

---

# Difference Between Internet and WWW

| Internet | WWW (World Wide Web) |
|----------|-----------------------|
| A global network of computers | A collection of websites on the Internet |
| Provides the connection | Provides web pages and websites |
| Works using different services | Uses HTTP and HTTPS protocols |
| Example: Internet connection | Example: Google website |

# Why is WWW Used?

The World Wide Web is used to access and share information easily over the Internet.

### Main Uses of WWW

## 1. To Access Websites
WWW allows users to open websites from anywhere in the world.

**Example:**
- Google
- Amazon
- Wikipedia

---

## 2. To Search Information
People use the web to find information on any topic.

**Example:**
- Educational notes
- News
- Tutorials

---

## 3. Online Learning
Students can learn through educational websites.

**Example:**
- Online courses
- Study materials
- Video lectures

---

## 4. Communication
WWW helps people communicate online.

**Examples:**
- Email
- Social media
- Chat applications

---

## 5. Online Shopping
People can buy products from websites.

**Examples:**
- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Myntra

---

## 6. Online Banking

Banks provide services through websites.

**Examples:**
- Check account balance
- Transfer money
- Pay bills

---

## 7. Entertainment

People can watch videos, listen to music, and play games.

**Examples:**
- YouTube
- Netflix
- Spotify

---

## 8. Business and Marketing

Companies use websites to promote and sell their products.

**Examples:**
- Company websites
- Online advertisements
- E-commerce stores

---

## 9. File Sharing

Users can upload and download files.

**Examples:**
- PDF files
- Images
- Software
- Documents

---

## 10. Government Services

Many government services are available through websites.

**Examples:**
- Online forms
- Tax payment
- Exam results
- Government portals
