# What is a Website?

A **website** is a collection of related **web pages** that are connected together and available on the Internet under a single **domain name**.

A website can contain **text, images, videos, audio, forms, and links**.

**Simple Definition:**
> **A website is a collection of web pages that can be accessed through the Internet using a web browser.**

**Example Websites:**
- https://www.google.com
- https://www.wikipedia.org
- https://www.amazon.com

---

# Why is a Website Used?

Websites are used to:

- Share information
- Sell products and services
- Learn online
- Communicate with people
- Promote businesses
- Provide entertainment
- Offer online services

---

# Types of Websites

## 1. Static Website

A **Static Website** displays fixed content. Every visitor sees the same information.

**Examples:**
- Personal portfolio
- Company profile
- Resume website

**Advantages:**
- Fast loading
- Easy to create
- Low cost

---

## 2. Dynamic Website

A **Dynamic Website** displays content that changes based on user actions or database information.

**Examples:**
- Facebook
- Amazon
- Gmail

**Advantages:**
- Interactive
- Easy to update
- Personalized content

---

## 3. E-commerce Website

Used to buy and sell products online.

**Examples:**
- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Myntra

**Features:**
- Shopping cart
- Online payment
- Product catalog

---

## 4. Business Website

Used by companies to promote their products and services.

**Examples:**
- Company websites
- Service provider websites

**Features:**
- About Us
- Services
- Contact Information

---

## 5. Educational Website

Provides educational content and online learning.

**Examples:**

- Notes
- Videos
- Exams

## 6. Blog Website

Used to publish articles and share knowledge.

**Examples:**

**Features:**

- Comments
- Categories

- Videos

## 8. Portfolio Website

## 9. Social Media Website

- Instagram

## 10. Government Website

Provides government information and online services.

**Examples:**

- Passport services
- Exam portals

## 11. Entertainment Website

Provides movies, music, games, and videos.

**Examples:**
- YouTube
- Netflix
- Spotify

---

## 12. Forum Website

Allows people to ask questions and discuss topics.

**Examples:**
- Discussion forums
- Community websites

## 13. Search Engine Website

Helps users search for information on the Internet.

**Examples:**

- Bing

## 14. Personal Website

Created by an individual to share personal information, hobbies, or achievements.
